> benchmarks, method, source, rapira.

Each chart shows the change of the new build of each run from one fixed baseline build. Every run measures the two builds one after the other on the same instances, so a point does not include the difference between two rigs. A point is the median of the paired deltas of its run. The measures are capacity in req/s, and p99 latency and RSS at a constant request rate per target. A higher capacity is better. A lower p99 and a lower RSS are better. A point is green for a better build and red for a worse build only when every round gave a pair, all the paired deltas are on one side of zero, and the delta reaches the noise floor. The noise floor is the largest paired delta of a run in which the two builds are the same build. The gRPC p99 has a higher noise floor. Other points are gray. A gap means a measurement is missing. The charts show only the runs that have the baseline build and the settings of the newest run. The tooltip includes the measured values of the two builds, and the smallest and the largest paired delta. A click opens the pull request, or the commit for a run without one. On a touch screen, a tap shows the tooltip, and a tap on a point opens the run.

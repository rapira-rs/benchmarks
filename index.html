> benchmarks, method, source, rapira.

Each chart shows the change from the first published build of this benchmark method. That build is the fixed baseline at 0%. The measures are capacity in req/s, and p99 latency and RSS at a constant request rate per target. A higher capacity is better. A lower p99 and a lower RSS are better. A run measured against another build gives a cumulative estimate through its recorded comparisons. Two successive gains of 10% give a total gain of 21%. These estimates are gray, have no spread band, and are labelled in the tooltip. Measurement noise can accumulate. Direct comparisons with the baseline show the smallest and largest paired change as a band. Their points are green for a better build and red for a worse build only when every round gave a pair, the band is on one side of zero, and the change reaches the noise floor. The gRPC p99 has a higher noise floor. Other points are gray. A gap means a measurement or a connection to the baseline is missing. The tooltip includes the original measured values and base commit. A click opens the pull request, or the commit for a run without one. On a touch screen, a tap shows the tooltip, and a tap on a point opens the run.

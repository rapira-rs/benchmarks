> benchmarks, method, source, rapira.

The p99 latency and the RSS of rapira at a constant request rate per target, for each nightly build of the rapira main branch, labelled with the merged pull request of its commit. Hover over a chart, or tap it on a touch screen, to see the values of a run and the change since the first point of the chart. A click opens the pull request, or the commit for a run without one. On a touch screen, a tap on a point opens it.
